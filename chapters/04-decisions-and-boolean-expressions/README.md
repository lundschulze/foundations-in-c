# Chapter 4 — Decisions and Boolean expressions

Companion code and exercises for Chapter 4 of *Foundations in C*.

## Material

- [Decision](examples/decision/) — select one of two branches with `if` and `else`
- [Boarding](examples/boarding/) — combine Boolean conditions with logical AND
- [Luggage decision](exercises/luggage-decision/) — build and test a three-way decision at its boundaries

The chapter introduces equality and relational operators, zero and non-zero conditions, `bool`, logical operators and nested decisions.
